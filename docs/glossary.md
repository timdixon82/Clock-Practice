# Project Wiki: Glossary: Clock-Practice

Domain terms specific to Clock-Practice, defined as the team meets them. Terms that apply across every project are in the global wiki's glossary.

## Analogue clock

A clock that shows the time with a numbered dial and rotating hands, rather than as digits.

## Hour hand

The shorter hand of an analogue clock. It points to the hour.

## Minute hand

The longer hand of an analogue clock. It points to the minute.
